This paper proposes using in-context sharpness as alerts for hallucination mitigation in LLMs. We show that the sharpness of in-context representations can serve as an effective indicator for detecting potential hallucinations.

**Authors:** Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He
