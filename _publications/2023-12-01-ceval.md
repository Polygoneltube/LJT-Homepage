This paper introduces C-Eval, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. C-Eval consists of multiple-choice questions across 52 diverse disciplines and four difficulty levels, providing a thorough assessment of model capabilities.

**Authors:** Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He
