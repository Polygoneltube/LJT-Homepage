This paper explores composing parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations. We demonstrate that arithmetic operations on parameter-efficient modules can effectively combine and transfer capabilities across different tasks.

**Authors:** Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junteng Liu, Junxian He
