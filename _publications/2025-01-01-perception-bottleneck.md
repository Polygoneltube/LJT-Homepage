This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) in chart understanding tasks. We identify key limitations in current VLMs' ability to accurately perceive and understand chart data, and propose solutions to address these bottlenecks.

**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

**Code:** [Vision4Chart](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Vision4Chart)
