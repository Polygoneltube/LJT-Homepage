This paper explores the existence of a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside Large Language Models. We demonstrate that there exists a direction in the representation space that consistently separates truthful from untruthful statements, providing insights into the internal mechanisms of LLMs.

**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He
